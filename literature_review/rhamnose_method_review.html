Project comparison. The current soft-sensor workflow treats Raman as the most direct carbohydrate signal for rhamnose, while EEM and PARAFAC are interpreted as indirect fluorescence/process-state inputs. HPLC remains the required quantitative reference method for culture samples. Literature evidence should therefore be used to justify spectroscopy preprocessing, model families, validation design, and the need for grouped culture-sample validation once quantitative HPLC targets are available.
