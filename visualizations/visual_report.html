Interpretive Summary

The Raman mean spectrum shows a limited number of consistently strong bands across the dataset. The most prominent regions are near 1156 cm-1 and 1523 cm-1, which are reasonable candidate features for downstream regression because they remain visible after averaging across hundreds of spectra.

The raw EEM mean map is dominated by very strong near-diagonal and saturated regions. Those features are visually impressive but scientifically risky to use directly because they are strongly confounded by detector saturation and optical scatter. The cleaned EEM panel below is therefore the more appropriate map for feature selection.
